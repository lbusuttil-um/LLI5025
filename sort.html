Activity 1

Rule-based or learned from data?

Some technology follows instructions a person wrote down. Some technology worked out its own way of deciding by looking at thousands or millions of examples. Telling the two apart is the first step to understanding what AI can and cannot do.

The nesting dolls

Each term sits inside the one before it. All generative AI is deep learning, all deep learning is machine learning, and all machine learning is AI. The reverse is not true.

Artificial intelligenceAny system that does something we would call intelligent if a person did it.
Includes old-style rule-based systems: a chess program from the 1980s, an expert system for diagnosing faults.
Machine learningThe system learns patterns from examples instead of being given rules.
A spam filter trained on emails that people marked as spam.
Deep learningMachine learning with large neural networks of many layers, good with images, sound and language.
Face unlock, voice typing, Google Translate.
Generative AIDeep learning models that produce new text, images, audio or code.
ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, image generators.

Your turn: sort these

For each technology, decide whether it works by following rules a programmer wrote, by learning from data, or a bit of both. Discuss in your group before you tap. Some of these are meant to cause an argument.
