Google Forms for Session 1

Four short forms. Build each in Google Forms, then paste its share link into assets/config.js. This page is not linked from the participant pages, but anyone with the address can open it, so leave it out of the repository if you prefer.

For every form: in Settings, turn off "Collect email addresses" and "Limit to 1 response" unless you want sign-in, so participants on tablets without a Google account can answer. For the two quizzes, turn on "Make this a quiz" and set answers to release after the session.
