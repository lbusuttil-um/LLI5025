Activity 3 · hands on

Train it, then break it

You will build a real image, sound or pose classifier for your subject in about fifteen minutes. Then you will spend the rest of the time trying to make it fail. The failures are the point: each one tells you something about how the model learned.

Getting started

  1. Open Teachable Machine and choose Image, Audio or Pose to match your card below. For Image, pick the standard image model.
  2. Rename each class with your labels. Add a class if your card has three.
  3. Collect examples for every class using the webcam or microphone. Hold the record button and move the object around so you get different angles.
  4. Press Train Model and keep the tab open while it trains. On a tablet this can take a little longer.
  5. Test it live in the Preview panel. Note how confident the model is for each class.
  6. Work through the four challenges below, then fill in the form.

Training happens in your browser and your images are not uploaded unless you choose to save the project to Google Drive. If you try this in school, check your school's policy before recording pupils' faces or voices.

Four ways to break it

Try each one. Record what happened to the confidence bars, not just whether the answer was right.

1

The stranger

Train with one person's hands, face or voice, then test with someone else in the group. Does it work equally well for everyone?

2

The new room

Change the background, the lighting or where you stand. Hold the object in front of a busy poster or a window.

3

The lopsided class

Start a fresh model with about 60 examples of one class and only 6 of another. Which class does it favour when it is unsure?

4

The impostor

Show it something that belongs to none of your classes. It has no way of saying "I don't know". How confident is its wrong answer?

Record what you found

One form per group: what you trained, what broke it, and why you think it broke. We will look at the best failures together.
