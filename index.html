Session 1 of 2

How AI works

Today we look under the bonnet. By the end you should be able to tell AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI apart, and explain from your own experience how a machine learning model learns from data, and why it sometimes learns the wrong thing.

Before you start

Devices

Everything here works on a laptop or tablet. For Teachable Machine, at least one device in your group needs a camera, and a laptop is easier for the pose activities.

Groups

Work in subject groups of three or four. Use the same group name every time you submit a form so we can follow your thinking across the session.
