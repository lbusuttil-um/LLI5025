Session 1 of 2

How AI works

Today we look under the bonnet. By the end you should be able to tell AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI apart, and explain from your own experience how a machine learning model learns from data, and why it sometimes learns the wrong thing.

Before you start

Devices

Everything here works on a laptop or tablet. For Teachable Machine you need to download a zip from Moodle, upload pictures and take a screenshot, so check you can do all three on your device.

Groups

Work in subject groups of three or four. When Slido asks for a name, use your group name so we can follow your thinking across the session.
